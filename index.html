Common rabbit breeds
Small · 2–4 lb

Netherland Dwarf

Small, energetic and alert. A calm approach and gentle handling are important.

Small · 3–4 lb

Holland Lop

Compact with distinctive folded ears and a generally friendly temperament.

Medium · 6–8 lb

Mini Rex

Known for its soft, velvet-like coat and a generally calm personality.

Medium · 6–10 lb

Lionhead

Recognizable by its wool mane, which benefits from regular grooming.

Giant · 13–20 lb

Flemish Giant

A large rabbit that needs considerably more living and exercise space.

Medium · 6–7.5 lb

English Angora

Its long wool requires frequent and careful coat maintenance.

Large · 9–12 lb

New Zealand Rabbit

A large rabbit with a broad, muscular body and upright ears. New Zealand rabbits are generally calm, friendly and easy to handle. Their larger size means they need plenty of living and exercise space.
